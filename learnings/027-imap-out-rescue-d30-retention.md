## Hypothesis

N/A — data observation on IMAP-Out Rescue user retention.

## Test Design

Measured D30 retention rates for IMAP-Out Rescue users by platform and email client.

## Outcome

D30 retention: Android Gmail users 17%, iOS Gmail users 11%. A 1-2% absolute increase in retention would lead to +35K DAU.

## Nuance

These retention rates represent the baseline — users who download the app via IMAP-Out Rescue and are still active 30 days later. The gap between Android (17%) and iOS (11%) may reflect different user populations or onboarding experiences. The team launched an onboarding experiment on iOS but few users were eligible; relaunched on Android with relaxed criteria. Even small retention improvements at this scale have massive DAU impact given the 20K-30K daily download volume.

## Hypothesis
Improving the email sharing flow on iOS will drive engagement and organic user acquisition.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: iOS
- **Project Type**: Engagement-Feature
- **Experiment Window**: Jun 22, 2024 – Aug 14, 2024
- **Decision**: Iterate (unable to GA due to lack of share retraction capability)

## Outcome
**+4,000 DAU** — positive signal, but blocked from GA by a missing feature (sharer can't retract shares).

## Nuance
Compare to Android Email Sharing [019] which also showed positive signals. The iOS version was iterated (not GA'd) because of a product gap: users couldn't retract shared emails. This is notable — a positive experiment was blocked not by poor results but by a missing safety feature. The team later ran Email Sharing v2 (Sender) and v2.5 (Receiver) to iterate on the experience.

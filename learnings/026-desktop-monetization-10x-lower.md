## Outcome

Engagement features contributed +70K incremental DAU — all on desktop, where per-user monetization is approximately 10x lower than mobile.

## Nuance

This has major strategic implications. Desktop DAU is worth significantly less in revenue terms. Growth initiatives that shift users from desktop to mobile (like IMAP-Out Rescue, which drives app downloads) have an outsized revenue impact compared to desktop-only engagement features. When prioritizing, a smaller mobile DAU gain may be worth more than a larger desktop gain. This doesn't mean desktop engagement is unimportant — but the revenue math should inform allocation decisions.

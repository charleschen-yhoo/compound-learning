## Hypothesis
An animated delete-by-sender (DbS) creative on the Landshark interstitial will increase app download conversion.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: Mobile Web (Noble)
- **Project Type**: Acquisition
- **Experiment Window**: Starting May 7, 2025
- **Decision**: Paused

## Outcome
**+18-19% increase in downloads** (iOS and Android respectively) — but paused due to revenue impact.

## Nuance
This is a critical tension: the animation significantly boosts downloads but negatively impacts revenue. The revenue loss likely comes from users who would have generated ad revenue on mobile web being redirected to app download (where they may churn or not immediately monetize). This is the same strategic tension as IMAP-Out broadly — acquiring mobile app users at the cost of current-session revenue. However, unlike IMAP-Out Rescue (which targets third-party client users with near-zero Yahoo revenue), Landshark targets mobile web users who ARE generating Yahoo revenue. The decision to pause rather than terminate suggests the team wants to find a way to capture the download lift without the revenue loss.

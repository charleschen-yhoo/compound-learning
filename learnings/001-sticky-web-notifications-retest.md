## Hypothesis

Making important desktop (Windows) notifications sticky — persisting until user interacts rather than auto-dismissing — will increase notification engagement and drive incremental desktop DAU.

## Test Design

A/B test over 14 days on Windows desktop. Control: non-sticky notifications (auto-dismiss). Treatment: sticky notifications for important emails only. 

## Outcome

+4,300 Desktop DAU. Notification opens/user: +190.5% (stat sig). Notification CTP: +10.6pp (stat sig). DV: +0.85% (stat sig). Expecting 2x impact with expansion to MacOS.

## Nuance

Only important notifications are sticky — not all notifications. This distinction likely matters for user tolerance. Windows-only results; MacOS expansion expected to roughly double the impact. The massive lift in opens/user suggests users wanted to engage but were missing the brief notification window.

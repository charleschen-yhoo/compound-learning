## Hypothesis
Email campaigns targeting lapsed users with a win-back message can drive reactivation.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: Email
- **Project Type**: Engagement-Feature
- **Decision**: Terminated

## Outcome
**+19,000 DAU** — positive signal, but terminated.

## Nuance
Like the User Resurrection Campaign [035] (113K DAU, paused), this email campaign showed positive numbers but wasn't scaled. The termination despite positive DAU suggests issues beyond topline impact — possibly low retention of reactivated users, high cost, or interference with other campaigns. The reactivation channel landscape now includes SMS [015] (ROI-positive by D2), email campaigns [035, this], and IMAP-Out logouts [012] — email campaigns appear to be the least favored despite decent DAU numbers.

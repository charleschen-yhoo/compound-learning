## Hypothesis

Expanding the IMAP-Out Rescue upsell to the password screen via a toast notification will increase app installs by reaching users at a new touchpoint.

## Test Design

Added a toast-style upsell ("Get the Yahoo Mail app for the best experience") on the password entry screen during IMAP-Out flows.

## Outcome

Total installs to date: 23,000+. CTR: 0.05%. Install rate: 33.3% (of those who clicked).

## Nuance

CTR is very low (0.05%) but install rate among clickers is high (33.3%), suggesting the message resonates with a small but motivated subset. This is an incremental surface — not a replacement for existing upsells. Next step is testing additional surface areas.

## Hypothesis
The IMAP-Out Rescue concept can be extended to desktop users, presenting them with both a mobile app download option and a desktop web engagement path.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: Identity / Web (Desktop)
- **Project Type**: Acquisition
- **Target Launch**: Nov 17, 2025
- **Decision**: GA

## Outcome
- **iPad downloads**: ~100/day
- **Desktop DAU**: +1,000/day

## Nuance
This is a novel extension of the IMAP-Out Rescue concept to a platform where you can't just redirect to an app store. The dual outcome (iPad downloads + desktop DAU) is interesting — some users encountering the rescue flow on desktop switch to iPad, while others stay on desktop web. The 1,000 desktop DAU is modest but represents users who were previously accessing Yahoo Mail through a third-party desktop client. Given desktop's ~10x lower monetization [026], the revenue impact is limited, but these users may migrate to mobile over time following the natural platform migration pattern seen in SMS resurrection [015].

## Hypothesis
An improved version of Tidy Inbox on Android will drive broader engagement beyond just Fanatic users (which was the limitation in the 1.5 Android test).

## Test Design
- **Platform**: Android
- **Project Type**: Engagement-Feature
- **Experiment Window**: Jan 9, 2025 – Feb 21, 2025
- **Decision**: GA

## Outcome
**+3,750 DAU** — much smaller than iOS 1.5 (96,510 DAU) but GA'd.

## Nuance
The 25x gap between iOS 1.5 (96,510 DAU) and Android 1.6 (3,750 DAU) is one of the starkest platform differences in the corpus. Even after iterating to broaden engagement beyond Fanatics, the Android version delivers a fraction of iOS impact. The iOS 1.6 version showed "no lift in DV" and was not GA'd — suggesting Tidy Inbox may have reached saturation on iOS while still scaling on Android. Cleanup Buddy [058] was later tested as a coupling with Tidy Inbox to boost engagement further.

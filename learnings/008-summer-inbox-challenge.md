## Hypothesis

A time-bounded 7-day triage challenge with rewards will lift engagement and drive incremental revenue on desktop.

## Test Design

Oasis desktop users in the US with 30+ emails. Users earn rewards by taking triage actions (reading, deleting, marking as read) over 7 days. Entry window: 8/21-9/5. Reward visible for 7 days post-completion. 45% of US Oasis users eligible.

## Outcome

Revenue: +$100K (8/21-9/19), +$127K (8/21-9/30) — revenue continued post-challenge. Engagement: PV +2.03%, Time Spent +1%, DV7 +0.7%. Treatment bucket continued to show revenue upside after challenge ended. Bucket-level PV lift of +2.03% despite 43% opt-out rate.

## Nuance

The +2.03% PV lift at bucket level with a 43% opt-out rate means the per-participant impact is much higher — the feature more than compensated for opt-outs. Revenue persisting after the challenge window suggests behavior change, not just temporary gamification lift. This was the proof point that led to recurring challenges (Haunted Inbox Challenge) and the broader motivational system strategy.

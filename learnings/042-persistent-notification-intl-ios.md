## Hypothesis
The persistent notification upsell (reminding users to enable notifications) will work for international (non en-US) users on iOS.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: iOS
- **Project Type**: Engagement-Notif
- **Experiment Window**: Oct 24, 2025 – Nov 7, 2025
- **Buckets**: 5% test, non en-US users only
- **Decision**: GA

## Outcome
**+20,000 DAU** — INTL users respond to persistent notification upsells on iOS.

## Nuance
Compare to the Android INTL version which showed +1.1% opt-in increase but neutral engagement — the iOS version was dramatically more effective. The Android version had an "extraneous eligibility param" bug that may explain the disparity. The iOS success here supports scaling notification upsell patterns internationally, but the Android result needs a clean rerun before drawing conclusions.

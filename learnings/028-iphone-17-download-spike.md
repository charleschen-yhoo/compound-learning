## Hypothesis

N/A — external event observation.

## Test Design

Monitored daily IMAP-Out Rescue downloads around the iPhone 17 release (9/19/2025).

## Outcome

Estimated 105K downloads from iPhone 17 users in the first 7 days after release. Visible as a spike in the daily downloads chart, jumping from ~32K/day baseline to ~55K peak.

## Nuance

New device launches create organic spikes in app downloads as users set up new phones and encounter the IMAP-Out Rescue flow. This is not directly controllable but is predictable and could be capitalized on — e.g., preparing optimized onboarding experiences or special creative for new device setup flows around known launch dates. The spike is temporary but contributes meaningful volume.

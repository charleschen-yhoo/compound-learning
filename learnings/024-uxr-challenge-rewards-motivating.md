## Hypothesis

Users will find the challenge reward mechanics engaging and motivating, creating a sense of accomplishment.

## Test Design

Qualitative UXR interviews with Summer Inbox Challenge participants + social media monitoring.

## Outcome

Three user quotes captured: (1) Olesia: enjoyed the challenge, participated 3 of 5 days, found the mini-tasks and trophies interesting and competitive. (2) Kia: the reward created a feeling of accomplishment even without completing the full challenge — "it will make you want to continue and keep going." (3) Jeanna: liked that the reward was easy to achieve — not too much work required. Social proof: Instagram post from user thanking Yahoo for "hyping me up for cleaning out my email."

## Nuance

This is qualitative, not quantitative — directional signal only. The key theme across all three users is that low-barrier rewards create motivation. Jeanna's feedback is especially notable: the reward felt achievable, not like a grind. This aligns with the quantitative finding that 50% of users who hit Milestone 1 complete the full challenge — the first win hooks them. Organic social sharing suggests genuine delight, not just compliance.

## Hypothesis

Understanding the challenge funnel drop-off points will identify where to optimize for higher completion.

## Test Design

Funnel analysis of Summer Inbox Challenge participants from eligibility through completion.

## Outcome

7.4M US Oasis users → 4.7M pass 30+ email filter (64%) → 2.7M remain opted in (57% of filtered, after 43% opt-out) → 1.0M reach Milestone 1 (37% of opt-in) → 0.7M reach Milestone 2 → 0.5M complete (18% of opt-in).

## Nuance

The key insight: 50% of users who hit Milestone 1 go on to complete the entire challenge. This suggests the first milestone is the critical activation point — once users experience the first reward, they're hooked. Optimization should focus on getting more users to Milestone 1, not on reducing drop-off later in the funnel. The biggest losses are at opt-out (43%) and pre-Milestone 1 (63% of opt-in users never reach it).

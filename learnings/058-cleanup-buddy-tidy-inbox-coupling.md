## Hypothesis
Cleanup Buddy (an AI-assisted inbox cleanup tool) will drive engagement, especially when coupled with Tidy Inbox.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: iOS (v7.74) + Android (v7.74)
- **Project Type**: Engagement-Feature
- **Experiment Window**: Oct 14-20, 2025 – Nov 4-10, 2025
- **Decision**: Iterate on both platforms — will iterate to get DV read

## Outcome
Cleanup Buddy coupled with Tidy Inbox showed **much stronger engagement** than standalone. No DV lift achieved yet — iterating.

## Nuance
The coupling insight is important: Cleanup Buddy works better when paired with Tidy Inbox than standalone. This suggests that users need both the "what to clean" (Tidy Inbox suggestions) and the "how to clean" (Cleanup Buddy tools) to change behavior. The earlier Cleanup Buddy [005] showed bulk delete as the strongest value prop but lost visibility in the Orbit migration. The iterate decision suggests engagement is there but DV impact needs further work — similar to the Tidy Inbox 1.5 Android pattern [037] where engagement was concentrated in a narrow segment.

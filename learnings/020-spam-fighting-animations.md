## Hypothesis

Testing new animation variants in the IMAP-Out Rescue upsell will improve download conversion.

## Test Design

Tested two new animation concepts against the default. Winning variant: "Unread count decreasing" animation. GA'd on 5/27/2025.

## Outcome

+22% lift in downloads. Helped consistently cross the 20K daily download threshold, averaging 24K downloads/day.

## Nuance

Creative iteration on the upsell animation is a meaningful growth lever. The "Unread count decreasing" concept resonates because it visualizes the core value proposition (inbox cleanup) in motion. This, combined with Lottie optimization (#017) and "Maybe Later" removal (#018), represents a compounding series of IMAP-Out Rescue optimizations that scaled downloads from ~5K/day to 24K+/day over 2025.

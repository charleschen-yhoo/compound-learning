## Hypothesis
Intercepting Apple Mail users who have Yahoo accounts connected via IMAP and presenting a rescue flow to download the Yahoo Mail app will drive meaningful app installs.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: Identity (cross-platform web flow)
- **Project Type**: Acquisition
- **Experiment Window**: Jul 15, 2024 – Jul 30, 2024
- **Decision**: GA

## Outcome
**+55,700 DAU** — the foundational IMAP-Out Rescue experiment that proved the entire acquisition pipeline.

## Nuance
This is the first Apple Mail IMAP-Out Rescue experiment — the one that validated the concept before the team invested in optimizations like Lottie animations [017], removing "Maybe Later" [018], and subsequent rescue versions (2.0, 2.5 [054]). The 55,700 DAU figure established the baseline that all subsequent IMAP-Out experiments built upon. Combined with the logout program [012], this formed the two-pronged IMAP-Out acquisition strategy.

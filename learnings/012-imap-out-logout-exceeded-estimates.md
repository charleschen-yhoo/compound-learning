## Hypothesis

Logging out IMAP-Out-Only users (those accessing Yahoo Mail exclusively via third-party email clients) would drive them to download the Yahoo Mail app, generating incremental DAU.

## Test Design

Phased logout campaigns targeting IMAP-Out-Only users. Originally sized the opportunity at 1.6M DAU for 2026 based on 75K/day replenishing pool at 6% yield.

## Outcome

Through April 12, 2026, logouts alone delivered +1.1M DAU — on pace to exceed 2.4M+ for the full year, 50% above the original estimate. Prime logouts (never previously logged out) maintain steady 90K/week at 6%+ yield with minimal churn, consistent with the 2025 compounding pattern. Re-logouts (2nd logout of the 40M+ pool) began in Feb 2026 and are now a meaningful contributor alongside prime logouts.

## Nuance

The IMAP-Out-Only segment is a proven compounder: 6% yield with no drop-off over time. This is unlike other segments which show leakage. The re-logout unlock (40M+ users who had already been logged out once) was a major expansion of the addressable pool. The program accounts for 90%+ of the 2026 DAU goal. 2H'25 generated $9.5M in incremental revenue from logout + other UG initiatives.

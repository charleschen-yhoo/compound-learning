## Hypothesis
Offering AI-generated quick reply suggestions on Android will lower the barrier to responding to emails, increasing replies per user.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: Android
- **Metric**: Replies/reply-all per user
- **Status**: GA'd

## Outcome
- **+3.5% stats sig lift in replies/reply-all per user**
- GA'd and launched

## Nuance
Quick Replies (+3.5%) and Emoji Reactions [072] (+2.97%) are complementary lightweight response mechanisms on Android. Together they suggest users have a strong appetite for faster, lower-effort email responses. The compounding effect of both features being GA'd should be monitored — if both are live simultaneously, the combined lift may be additive or there may be substitution effects.

The iOS Quick Replies experiment was in the "Now" pipeline at time of deck, suggesting cross-platform validation was planned. If iOS shows a similar lift, this would be a high-confidence pattern that lightweight response options drive engagement across platforms.

This result, combined with the Floating Compose failure [073], sharpens the "writing" strategy: users don't need help finding the compose button — they need help reducing the effort of responding.

## Hypothesis

Understanding when users opt out of challenges will inform whether the experience is intrusive and how to optimize the opt-out flow.

## Test Design

Analyzed opt-out timing distribution across the 7-day challenge window for the Summer Inbox Challenge.

## Outcome

98% of all opt-outs happen on Day 0 — the day users are introduced to the experience. After Day 1, opt-out rate drops to ~1% and effectively zero for Days 2-7.

## Nuance

This strongly suggests the experience is not intrusive to users who give it even one day. The high Day 0 opt-out is a snap reaction, not an informed rejection. Delaying the initial opt-out popup would likely rescue users who are on the fence — they'd experience the feature before being prompted to leave, and the data shows almost no one leaves after Day 0.

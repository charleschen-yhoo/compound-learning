## Hypothesis
Redesigning push notifications to prominently feature the sender name/identity will increase notification engagement.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: iOS
- **Project Type**: Engagement-Notif
- **Decision**: GA

## Outcome
**+106,000 DAU** — showing sender identity makes notifications more personally relevant and drives opens.

## Nuance
This confirms that notification content quality matters as much as notification delivery. Users are more likely to act on a notification when they can quickly assess "who sent this?" Aligns with the broader pattern that relevance filtering (important emails [033], sender identity [this]) outperforms volume increases. The notification stack becomes: delivery reliability [032] → relevance/quality [033, 036] → frequency tuning [034] → engagement features [001].

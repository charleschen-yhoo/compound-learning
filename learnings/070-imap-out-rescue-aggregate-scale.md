## Outcome
**2025 full year:**
- **8.6M app downloads** driven by IMAP-Out Rescue
- **1.3M incremental DAU** attributed to the program
- **Pacing to 10M+ downloads in 2026**

**Key learnings to date:**
- Users respond to video content (animations, Lottie [017])
- Users care about inbox management and SPAM messaging (bulk delete value prop [005])

**Context:** Login page receives 1M+ impressions/day from third-party email clients. Users often don't know about Yahoo's native apps or believe they are already using the Yahoo Mail app.

## Nuance
The 8.6M → 1.3M DAU conversion (15% download-to-DAU rate) is consistent with the D30 retention data: 17% Android, 11% iOS [027]. This validates the retention numbers at program scale.

The 1M+ daily login page impressions from third-party clients represents the total addressable market for IMAP-Out Rescue. At current download rates (~24K+/day), the program converts roughly 2.4% of daily IMAP impressions to downloads. There may be significant headroom, or the remaining 97.6% may be users who have already been exposed and declined.

The pacing to 10M+ in 2026 suggests the program is still scaling, not plateauing — surface expansion (Gmail [055], desktop [056]) and creative optimization (animations [063]) continue to add incremental volume.

## Hypothesis
Optimized animations (building on Lottie learnings [017]) applied specifically to the Gmail IMAP-Out Rescue flow will increase downloads.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: Identity (iOS + Android Gmail)
- **Project Type**: Acquisition
- **Experiment Window**: Apr 11, 2025 – May 2, 2025
- **Decision**: GA

## Outcome
- **iOS Gmail**: Flat impact on downloads
- **Android Gmail**: +6.4% lift in downloads

## Nuance
The iOS/Android split is striking — identical creative change, same Gmail rescue flow, dramatically different results. On iOS, the animation didn't move downloads at all, while Android saw a meaningful 6.4% lift. This could reflect differences in how animations render or how users interact with interstitials on each platform. The broader animation optimization pattern (Lottie [017]: +5.7% Android, Spam-Fighting [020]: creative lift) consistently shows stronger Android response to visual creative. The iOS animation story may need a fundamentally different approach.

## Hypothesis
Updating the Message Read view (version 2.5) with improved affiliate link presentation will increase affiliate revenue and click-through rates without degrading the reading experience.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: Not specified (likely cross-platform)
- **Metric**: Affiliate revenue, affiliate clicks
- **Status**: GA'd

## Outcome
- **+58.8% lift in affiliate revenue**
- **+95.29% lift in affiliate clicks**
- GA'd and launched

## Nuance
This is a rare monetization-focused win from the RWS Squad, which primarily targets engagement metrics. The magnitude of the lifts (58.8% revenue, 95.29% clicks) suggests the previous affiliate integration was significantly underperforming — the 2.5 update likely improved visibility, placement, or relevance of affiliate links in the message read view.

The near-doubling of clicks with a ~60% revenue increase implies the additional clicks convert at a somewhat lower rate than the baseline (more volume, slightly lower quality), which is typical when expanding ad surface. The team should monitor whether this level of affiliate integration causes user experience complaints over time.

This demonstrates that "reading" improvements can serve both engagement and revenue goals simultaneously — better presentation of commercial emails benefits both the user (easier to find deals) and the business (more affiliate revenue).

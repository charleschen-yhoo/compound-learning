## Hypothesis
A Halloween-themed inbox challenge ("The Haunted Inbox") will drive engagement and revenue similarly to the Summer Inbox Challenge.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: Desktop Web (Oasis)
- **Experiment Window**: Oct 16 – Nov 3, 2025
- **Comparison**: Side-by-side with Summer Inbox Challenge metrics

## Outcome
| Metric | Haunted Inbox | Summer Inbox |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| Page Views | +2.70% | +2.03% |
| DV | +1.93% | +0.69% |
| Ad Revenue | +1.63% | +1.24% |
| Delete | +6.19% | +1.73% |
| Read | Flat | +3.16% |
| Open | Flat | +1.73% |

**Total incremental revenue: $143K** (Oct 16 – Nov 3)

## Nuance
Haunted and Summer drove fundamentally different behaviors despite similar mechanics. Summer drove broad engagement across all actions (read, open, delete). Haunted drove a massive **delete surge (+6.19%)** — essentially gamifying "Spring Cleaning" of the inbox — while reads and opens were flat. This suggests the theme/framing matters: "Haunted" evoked cleanup, while "Summer" evoked general inbox engagement.

Haunted outperformed Summer on every topline metric (PV, DV, revenue) despite the narrower behavioral change. The +1.93% DV vs +0.69% suggests delete-heavy behavior drives more return visits (users who clean their inbox come back to keep it clean).

Notable: the challenges bucket drove **40% more clicks to the Mail+ CTA** compared to control. The team hypothesizes challenges create incremental impressions in that portion of the inbox, and Mail+ can capitalize. This is an unexpected revenue synergy.

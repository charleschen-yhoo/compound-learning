## Hypothesis
Enabling web push notifications on desktop (Norrin) will re-engage users who aren't actively in their inbox, driving incremental DAU.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: Desktop (Norrin)
- **Project Type**: Engagement-Notif
- **Experiment Window**: Jul 22, 2024 – Sep 2, 2024
- **Decision**: GA

## Outcome
**+267,000 DAU** — by far the single largest engagement experiment in the corpus. GA'd after clear positive signal.

## Nuance
This is the foundational web notification deployment that all subsequent desktop notification experiments (upsells, sticky notifications, reactivation notifications) build upon. The 267K DAU figure represents the total addressable impact of the notification channel on desktop — subsequent experiments optimize within this envelope.

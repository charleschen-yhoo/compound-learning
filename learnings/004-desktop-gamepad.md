## Hypothesis

Desktop users would benefit from a focused, lightweight triage flow similar to Gamepad on mobile, offering a faster and more satisfying experience with minimal cognitive overload.

## Test Design

Web Gamepad triggers for users with 25+ unread emails, displayed at most once per 14 days. Compared against control with no Gamepad experience.

## Outcome

No metric impact. Only 1.35% of treatment users ever tried Gamepad, driven by a single passive entry point. Of those who tried, 18.1% completion rate — much lower than iOS (32%) and Android (69%).

## Nuance

The feature wasn't disproved — it was barely discovered. The core issue is discoverability and entry points: mobile Gamepad relies on local notifications as a primary entry point, driving strong feature retention rates. Desktop doesn't have this mechanism. Deprioritized based on lack of engagement signal and entry point limitations. This suggests desktop engagement features need active, prominent entry points rather than passive ones.

## Hypothesis

Streaks v2 — using mechanics closer to the eventual evergreen program (user-set goals, 21-day duration, journeys vs streaks variations) — will deliver bucket-wide incremental DAU.

## Test Design

Active users (10+ unreads or 100+ total emails). Users pick a daily triage goal and receive credit for each day completed. Two variations: Journeys experience and Streaks experience. 21-day duration imitating eventual evergreen program.

## Outcome

Delivered bucket-wide incremental DAU. Stat sig lifts: pageviews +0.90%, sessions +0.76%. DV: +0.18% (stat sig via one method, borderline via another). P-value adjusted results confirmed pageviews and sessions lifts.

## Nuance

v2 substantiated v1's signal using mechanics closer to the final product. The pageview and session lifts are meaningful at bucket-wide scale even though DV lift is small. The 21-day duration confirms engagement lift can be sustained over a longer period, validating the evergreen program approach.

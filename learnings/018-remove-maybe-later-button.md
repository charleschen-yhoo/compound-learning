## Hypothesis

Removing the "Maybe Later" button from the IMAP-Out upsell will simplify the UI, drive stronger decision-making, and align the experience with actual product behavior (there is no follow-up — "Maybe Later" is misleading).

## Test Design

Default (with "Maybe Later" dismiss button) vs Challenger (button removed) on iOS IMAP-Out Rescue flow.

## Outcome

+16% lift in downloads (+808 per day). +22% lift in button CTR on iOS. -6% drop in App Store page conversion rate. Net positive: 204K incremental downloads projected by EOY → 58.4K DAU contribution.

## Nuance

Removing the easy dismissal forces a binary choice (download or close the screen entirely), which increases commitment. The -6% App Store conversion drop is expected — some users who previously would have dismissed now click through but aren't fully committed. But the net effect is strongly positive because far more users reach the App Store page. The "Maybe Later" framing was also dishonest — there was no planned follow-up, so removing it aligns UX with reality.

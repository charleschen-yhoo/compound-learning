## Hypothesis

A seamless email sharing experience on Android will increase sharing behavior and drive app downloads from shared email receivers.

## Test Design

Launched share feature on Android (previously iOS-only). Measured lift in share/forward behavior and incremental app downloads from receivers.

## Outcome

+0.94% lift in message openers using Share + Forward. +5.6% lift in messages shared and/or forwarded per message opener. 80 incremental downloads every day → 20K downloads/year contribution.

## Nuance

Email sharing can generate a steady stream of organic app downloads — small per day but consistent. The forward intercept may shift behavior (users share via link instead of forward) but doesn't increase overall sharing volume. The feature needed v3 for GA with email expiration support for legal/privacy requirements.

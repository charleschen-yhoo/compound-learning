## Hypothesis

The 30+ email eligibility threshold for challenges may be too restrictive, excluding users who would benefit most.

## Test Design

Analysis of the Summer Inbox Challenge eligibility funnel and cross-referencing with segment-level impact data.

## Outcome

Of 7.4M US Oasis users, only 4.7M passed the 30+ email filter. In the test bucket, 2.7M users (36%) were never shown challenges because they didn't meet the threshold. Meanwhile, O/T users (who tend to have fewer emails) showed the highest engagement lifts.

## Nuance

The initial threshold was set conservatively — worried users with too few emails wouldn't get the full experience. But given that O/T users respond disproportionately well to challenges, and the sheer volume excluded by the filter, relaxing to 5+ messages is expected to yield even greater results. The Haunted Inbox Challenge lowered this threshold.

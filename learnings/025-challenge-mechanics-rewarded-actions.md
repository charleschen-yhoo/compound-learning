## Hypothesis

Game mechanics in challenges will directly incentivize specific user behaviors, not general engagement.

## Test Design

Summer Inbox Challenge rewards triage actions: reading, deleting, and opening emails. Measured lift across all email actions (including non-rewarded ones like archive, spam, mark unread).

## Outcome

Stat sig lifts only on rewarded actions — Page Views +2.03%, Delete +1.73%, Read +3.16%, Open +1.31%. Non-rewarded actions showed no stat sig lift: Archive +1.71% (not sig), Spam +1.00% (not sig), Mark Unread +0.48% (not sig).

## Nuance

The game mechanics are precisely targeted — they drive exactly the behaviors they reward, and nothing else. This is both a strength (predictable, controllable) and a limitation (won't create broad engagement lift beyond the incentivized actions). If you want to drive a specific behavior, attach a reward to it. If you want general engagement lift, you need a different mechanism. This has direct implications for designing future challenge mechanics.

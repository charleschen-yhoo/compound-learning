## Hypothesis

Reducing friction for users to regain access to their accounts would drive incremental DAU.

## Test Design

Account Recovery initiative — specific mechanics not detailed in deck. Tracked as a category alongside IMAP-Out Rescue, Logout Campaigns, and Other Growth Initiatives.

## Outcome

Account Recovery resulted in minimal incremental DAU. Reported as "-" in the July 2025 squad charter (vs 511K from IMAP-Out Rescue, 363K from Logouts, 19.7K from Other). By September 2025, 272K DAU attributed to Account Recovery (up from zero), but this was after pivoting from Digital ID Verification.

## Nuance

Account Recovery as a category has underperformed relative to IMAP-Out and Engagement initiatives. The team correctly deprioritized it in favor of higher-yield opportunities. However, the 272K figure in the Jan 2026 charter suggests some later efforts may have found traction. The category may be worth revisiting once higher-yield initiatives plateau.

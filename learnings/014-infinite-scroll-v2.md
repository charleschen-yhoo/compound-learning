## Hypothesis

Reducing friction in the email reading experience by enabling faster infinite scroll (next message loads sooner) will increase engagement and topline metrics.

## Test Design

Variants: Control, V1 experience, 25% faster than V1, 50% faster than V1. V1 had initially shown +0.04% PV lift (not stat sig) with a conservative trigger (only 1.5% of scrolls loaded next message). V2 increased sensitivity.

## Outcome

Message Open lifted +1.25% (25% faster, stat sig) and +2.84% (50% faster, stat sig). Feature engagement: +3.30% and +4.60%. However, no stat sig impact on Revenue (-0.12% to +0.18%), DV (-0.14% to +0.01%), or Time Spent (-0.11% to +0.10%).

## Nuance

Users engage more with infinite scroll — they open more messages and interact with the feature. But this doesn't translate to topline metrics. The engagement is redistributed (users read through scroll instead of other navigation) rather than additive. Faster load speeds improved the experience but didn't create new sessions or revenue. This is a quality-of-life improvement, not a growth lever.

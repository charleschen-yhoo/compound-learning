## Hypothesis
Optimizing the network layer for inactivity notifications on Android will improve delivery reliability and drive incremental DAU.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: Android
- **Project Type**: Engagement-Notif
- **Decision**: GA

## Outcome
**+170,000 DAU** — second-largest engagement experiment in the corpus. Infrastructure-level optimization yielded massive returns.

## Nuance
This is a performance/infrastructure experiment, not a UX change. The result reinforces the same pattern seen in IMAP-Out Lottie optimization [017]: technical performance improvements can have outsized user-facing impact. Notification delivery reliability is a prerequisite for all other notification optimization work.

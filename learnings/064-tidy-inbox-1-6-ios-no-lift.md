## Hypothesis
An improved Tidy Inbox 1.6 on iOS will build on the 1.5 success (96,510 DAU [037]) and drive additional DV.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: iOS
- **Project Type**: Engagement-Feature
- **Experiment Window**: May 9, 2025 – May 30, 2025
- **Decision**: Need to rethink experiment setup

## Outcome
**No lift in DV.** Team noted they need to rethink how to set up the experiment.

## Nuance
After Tidy Inbox 1.5 delivered 96,510 DAU on iOS [037], the 1.6 iteration showed zero lift. This suggests either: (1) Tidy Inbox 1.5 already captured the addressable opportunity on iOS, (2) the 1.6 changes were incremental and didn't unlock a new user segment, or (3) the experiment methodology needs adjustment (as the team noted). Android 1.6 [052] did GA at +3,750 DAU, suggesting the Android market still had headroom. The Tidy Inbox trajectory on iOS may be hitting diminishing returns — prompting the pivot to Cleanup Buddy coupling [058].

## Hypothesis

Digital ID Verification would help users regain access to their accounts, driving incremental DAU through the account recovery funnel.

## Test Design

Details not in deck. Mentioned across multiple squad charters as an account recovery initiative.

## Outcome

Initial results showed negative ROI. Team pivoted away from the initiative.

## Nuance

Limited detail available in the MPU slides — this is noted as a strategic pivot rather than a detailed readout. The Account Recovery category as a whole generated minimal incremental DAU (marked as "-" in July 2025 charter). This led to deprioritizing account recovery in favor of higher-yield initiatives like IMAP-Out and engagement features.

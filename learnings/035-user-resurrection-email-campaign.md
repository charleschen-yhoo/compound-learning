## Hypothesis
Email campaigns targeting dormant/churned users can bring them back to Yahoo Mail at scale.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: Email
- **Project Type**: Engagement-Feature
- **Decision**: Paused (not GA'd or terminated — put on hold)

## Outcome
**+113,000 DAU** — large initial impact, but decision was to pause rather than GA.

## Nuance
Despite strong DAU numbers, the campaign was paused rather than GA'd. This could indicate concerns about sustainability, cost, or long-term retention of resurrected users. Compare to SMS Resurrection [015] which was ROI-positive by Day 2 but had only 0.2% sustained yield by D45. Email campaigns may show similar early spike / low retention dynamics. The paused status suggests the team saw enough signal to not terminate but not enough to scale.

## Hypothesis
Prompting or enabling users to set Yahoo Mail as their default mail app on iOS will increase engagement by capturing email intents from other apps.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: iOS
- **Project Type**: Engagement-Feature
- **Experiment Window**: Oct 15, 2024 – Nov 25, 2024
- **Decision**: GA

## Outcome
**+28,000 DAU** — setting Yahoo Mail as default on iOS drives meaningful daily engagement.

## Nuance
iOS 14+ allows third-party default mail apps. This is a structural engagement lever — once set, every "mailto:" link and email-related action across the OS routes to Yahoo Mail. The 28K DAU lift comes not from changing Yahoo Mail's product but from capturing ambient email intent on the device. This is a one-time setting change with compounding returns, similar to the notification opt-in funnel [016].

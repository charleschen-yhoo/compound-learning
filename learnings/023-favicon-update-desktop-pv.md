## Hypothesis

Updating the favicon (browser tab icon) would increase user engagement on desktop.

## Test Design

Details not fully documented in deck. Mentioned in the 2H Revenue Contribution waterfall.

## Outcome

+2.2% lift in bucket-wide PVs on desktop.

## Nuance

A notably low-effort change that delivered meaningful PV lift. Opened a "new medium" for the team to explore — small visual/brand touchpoints in the browser chrome that passively influence engagement. This suggests there may be other underexplored low-cost desktop optimizations in browser-level surfaces (tab titles, badge counts, etc.).

## Hypothesis
Adding a bottom sheet UX to the Apple Mail IMAP-Out Rescue flow (improving on v2's flat download impact) will increase download conversion.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: Identity (cross-platform web flow)
- **Project Type**: Acquisition
- **Experiment Window**: Jan 9, 2025 – Jan 15, 2025
- **Decision**: GA
- **Note**: v2 had +37% CTR from GIF variant but flat downloads; v2.5 addressed the conversion gap

## Outcome
**+24% to downloads** — driven by bottom sheet CTR improvement.

## Nuance
The progression tells a clear optimization story: v1 proved the concept (55,700 DAU [038]), v2 improved CTR with GIF but didn't convert to downloads, v2.5 fixed the download conversion with bottom sheet UX. Each iteration targeted a different funnel bottleneck. The +24% is on top of the v1 baseline, so the absolute download increase is substantial. This confirms the IMAP-Out team's systematic approach: measure the full funnel, identify the specific drop-off, and iterate on that step.

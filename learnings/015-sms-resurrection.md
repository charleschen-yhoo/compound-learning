## Hypothesis

Sending SMS reminders to inactive users (30+ days) about upcoming mailbox deletion will drive reactivation at positive ROI.

## Test Design

Contacted 64.8K US-only inactive users via SMS at $0.0031/message (total cost: $201). Users inactive for 30-90 days. Tracked incremental DAU and revenue over 45 days.

## Outcome

D0: 2K incremental DAUs (3.3% yield) — enough activity to payback the $201 SMS cost by Day 2. Long-term: gains muted to 114 DAUs (0.2% yield) by D45. Total revenue: $498. By D45, 80% of SMS DAUs are on mobile, with iOS app being the plurality (44%). On D0, plurality starts on mobile web (42%).

## Nuance

Extremely cheap channel with positive ROI. The platform shift is interesting: users initially respond via mobile web (path of least resistance from an SMS link) but those who stick migrate to the iOS app. Hypothesis: including an App Store link in the SMS could short-circuit this migration and unlock a new acquisition channel. The challenge is automation and legal/privacy compliance for automated transactional SMS sends.

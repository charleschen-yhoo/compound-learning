## Hypothesis
Updating the message read toolbar on Android (making reply/reply-all/forward actions more prominent or accessible) will increase reply behavior.

## Test Design
- **Platform**: Android
- **Metric**: Replies/reply-all/forwards per user
- **Status**: GA'd

## Outcome
- **+1.8% stats sig lift in replies/reply-all/forwards per user**
- GA'd and launched

## Nuance
A smaller lift than Quick Replies [077] (+3.5%) or Emoji Reactions [072] (+2.97%), but notable because it's a pure UX/layout change — no new functionality, just better affordance for existing actions. This suggests the reply actions were somewhat hidden or had unnecessary friction in the previous toolbar layout.

The pattern across the three Android "writing" experiments — toolbar (+1.8%), quick replies (+3.5%), emoji reactions (+2.97%) — shows that multiple small improvements compound. Each individually is modest, but together they represent a meaningful shift in reply engagement.

All three target the same metric (replies per user) on the same platform (Android), so the team should watch for diminishing returns as the most willing-to-reply users are already activated by earlier features.

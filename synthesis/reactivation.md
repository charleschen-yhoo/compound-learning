# Reactivation & Dormant Users

## Summary

The team has five tested reactivation channels: SMS, IMAP-Out logouts, email campaigns, web reactivation notifications, and win-back emails. SMS [015] is the cheapest and fastest-payback channel. IMAP-Out logouts [012] have the highest sustained yield. Email campaigns [035, 043] show large initial DAU but haven't been scaled. Web reactivation notifications [044] offer a unique logged-out re-engagement capability on desktop. Account recovery initiatives [022, 029] have consistently underperformed.

## Confidence-Ranked Findings

**High Confidence:**
- SMS resurrection is ROI-positive by Day 2 at $0.003/message [015]
- Users reached via SMS start on mobile web (42% D0) but shift to iOS app (44% D45) [015]
- Logout of IMAP-Out-Only users: 6% sustained yield, compounding [012]
- Account Recovery initiatives (Digital ID Verification): negative or minimal ROI [022, 029]
- Web reactivation notifications: +10,435 PVs/day, +4% logged-out CTP on desktop [044]

**Medium Confidence:**
- User Resurrection Email Campaign: +113,000 DAU but paused [035]
- Win-Back Email Campaign: +19,000 DAU but terminated [043]
- Email campaigns show strong initial signal but neither has been scaled — possible sustainability concerns
- Adding App Store links to SMS could accelerate the mobile web → app migration [015]

## Contradictions
- **Email campaigns show large DAU** (113K [035], 19K [043]) but **neither was scaled**, while **SMS at much lower DAU** was validated as ROI-positive [015]. This suggests email campaign DAU may be inflated by transient re-engagement that doesn't persist, while SMS's smaller but tracked cohort showed sustainable behavior change.

## Open Questions
- Can SMS be automated at scale? Legal/privacy compliance is the blocker.
- Why were email campaigns paused/terminated despite strong DAU numbers? Is it retention, cost, or cannibalization?
- What other SMS use cases exist beyond dormancy? (e.g., App Store CTA, time-based LandShark)
- Is there a re-engagement channel for desktop-only dormant users beyond web notifications?
- Can web reactivation notifications expand to MacOS to double the logged-out re-engagement pool?

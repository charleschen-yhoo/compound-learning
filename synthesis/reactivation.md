# Reactivation & Dormant Users

## Summary

The team has two active channels for reactivating dormant users: SMS and IMAP-Out logouts. Both are positive-ROI. SMS is extremely cheap ($0.003/message) with fast payback but the long-term yield is low (0.2% by D45). Logouts have high sustained yield for the IMAP-Out-Only segment (6%) but lower for O&O (2.5%).

## Confidence-Ranked Findings

**High Confidence:**
- SMS resurrection is ROI-positive by Day 2 at $0.003/message [015]
- Users reached via SMS start on mobile web (42% D0) but shift to iOS app (44% D45) — suggesting a natural platform migration [015]
- Logout of IMAP-Out-Only users is a compounding reactivation channel with 6% sustained yield [012]

**Medium Confidence:**
- Account Recovery initiatives (including Digital ID Verification) have underperformed — negative or minimal ROI [022, 029]
- Adding App Store links to SMS could accelerate the mobile web → app migration [015]

## Open Questions
- Can SMS be automated at scale? Legal/privacy compliance is the blocker.
- What other SMS use cases exist beyond dormancy? (e.g., App Store CTA, time-based LandShark)
- Is there a re-engagement channel for desktop-only dormant users?

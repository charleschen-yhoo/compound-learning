# New Users & Activation

## Summary

This is the least-developed theme with only one learning. The team recently discovered that new user funnels are heterogeneous — YMail-first users and Gmail SIWG users have different needs and expectations — but didn't previously have tracking to differentiate them. Registration volume is declining but activation rate is rising.

## Confidence-Ranked Findings

**Medium Confidence:**
- Daily registrations declining (85.6K → 67.8K, Jan-Apr 2026) but activation rate increasing (72.4% → 78.1%) [021]
- Gmail SIWG users arrive with a fundamentally different product expectation than new YMail users [021]

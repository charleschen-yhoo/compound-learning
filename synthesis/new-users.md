# New Users & Activation

## Summary

New users represent the smallest growth lever (0.1% of DAU, ~57K/day [068]) and the most structurally constrained. The team has identified three categories of constraints — impact, infrastructure, and data [069] — that explain why onboarding experiments consistently fail. Two specific onboarding experiments (newsletter recommendations [066], add a mailbox upsell [067]) both failed, reinforcing that post-hoc nudges can't overcome the fundamental empty inbox problem. Registration volume is declining but activation rate is rising [021]. The rational conclusion: the team has correctly deprioritized onboarding in favor of higher-leverage areas.

## Confidence-Ranked Findings

**High Confidence:**
- New accounts = 0.1% of DAU (57K/day) — growth is fundamentally a retention game, not an acquisition game [068]
- D7 retention: 12%, D90: 7% — even doubling D90 = only 200K incremental DAU [069]
- Onboarding is triple-constrained: impact (small funnel), infra (Evaluate can't support experiments, journey spans 3 properties), data (unknown user source, no JTBD signal, empty inbox) [069]
- Newsletter subscription recommendations failed — 0.3% signup rate, perceived as Yahoo ad [066]
- Add a mailbox upsell failed — 0.2% conversion despite 6-15% CTR; high-effort action can't be nudged from a TOI card [067]
- 35%+ of registration traffic comes from "unknown" source identified as IMAP [069]

**Medium Confidence:**
- Daily registrations declining (85.6K → 67.8K, Jan-Apr 2026) but activation rate increasing (72.4% → 78.1%) [021]
- Gmail SIWG users arrive with a fundamentally different product expectation than new YMail users [021]
- Mobile new user retention (44%) is dramatically higher than desktop (18%) [068]

## Contradictions
- **Registration volume is declining** [021] but **activation rate is rising** [021]. Fewer users are signing up, but those who do are more likely to stick. This could mean the casual/accidental registrations are dropping off while intentional users remain — a healthy normalization.

## Open Questions

- Is there a way to solve the empty inbox problem that doesn't rely on Yahoo content or post-hoc nudges?
- Can the onboarding infrastructure constraints be addressed, or is the ROI too low given the 0.1% DAU contribution?
- Should the team accept onboarding as structurally constrained and redirect all investment to retention/engagement of existing users?
